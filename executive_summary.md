# Executive Summary - France Top 50 Playlist Analysis

**Prepared for:** Atlantic Recording Corporation senior management

## Purpose
To understand how explicit and clean content, release format, song length and release size perform in the French market, so that France-specific release decisions rest on evidence.

## Data used
555 daily snapshots of the France Top 50 playlist (2024-05-18 to 2025-11-27) - 27,750 entries covering 725 songs by 324 artists. The data was checked and cleaned first (a duplicated day and a few invalid values were fixed; the original file was not changed).

## Main findings
- **Explicit content is common, not unusual.** 56.3% of entries are explicit, and 62.4% of the Top 10 entries. The share moved between roughly 45% and 67% from month to month.
- **No clear winner between explicit and clean.** Clean songs have higher popularity scores; explicit songs sit slightly higher in the chart. The chart difference is small.
- **Format:** album tracks hold slightly more places (52.9% vs 47.1%), but singles do better - higher popularity and a better average position - and stay on the chart longer.
- **Song length:** the best-placed songs are mostly 2.5 to 4 minutes long (85% of the Top 10). Songs over 4 minutes are less common at the top.
- **Album size:** songs from larger releases tend to score lower, mainly because singles (one-track releases) perform well. Among albums alone, the effect is weak.

## Compliance findings
The data contains only an explicit-content flag - nothing on age ratings, broadcast rules or listener sensitivity. Within that limit, explicit content does not prevent songs from reaching and holding top places in France.

## Format findings
Singles are the stronger performers; albums still provide more than half of all chart places.

## Duration findings
Target roughly 2.5-4 minutes. There is no evidence for an exact "perfect" length.

## Album structure findings
A pattern of weaker results for larger releases exists, but it is modest and should not be read as proof that more tracks cause weaker songs.

## Recommendations
**High confidence**
- Do not remove explicit content in order to qualify for the French chart.
- Aim for songs of about 2.5-4 minutes.
- Use singles or focus tracks to build popularity and reach the Top 10.

**Moderate confidence**
- Keep a clean version available as a low-cost option.
- Pitch both explicit and clean material; emphasise single-led, medium-length tracks for top positions.
- For albums, concentrate promotion on a few focus tracks.

**Further data needed:** streaming counts, skip and completion rates, genre and language, listener demographics, marketing spend, release timing, artist-level popularity, and playlists from other countries (to see what is specific to France).

## Limitations
This is one playlist over about 18 months. Songs appear on many days, so results are less certain than the raw counts suggest. Popularity and rank are platform measures, not streaming volumes. Results show associations, not causes.
